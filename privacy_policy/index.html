Sudoku Solver

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sudoku Solver ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Sudoku Solver is a straightforward Sudoku puzzle-solving application. We want to be transparent about the information we collect:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • No Usage Data: We do not track how you use the application or collect analytics data about your interactions with our app.
  • No Device Information: We do not collect information about your device, operating system, or device identifiers.
  • Puzzle Data: Sudoku puzzles you enter are processed locally on your device only. We do not transmit, store, or save any puzzle data to our servers.

3. How We Use Information

Since Sudoku Solver does not collect personal information, we have no information to use. All puzzle-solving occurs locally on your device using our algorithm, and no data is transmitted to external servers.

4. Data Security

Your privacy and security are our priorities. Since we do not collect or store any personal data:

  • All puzzle inputs are processed locally on your device.
  • No data is transmitted over the internet from the application.
  • Your Sudoku puzzles and solutions exist only on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Sudoku Solver does not integrate with any third-party services that collect personal data. The application functions entirely offline (except for initial download from app stores) and does not connect to external servers for data collection.

6. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Solver is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no special privacy protections needed for children. The application is safe for use by anyone.
